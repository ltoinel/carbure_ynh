Carbure is a self-hosted household budget manager: your bank accounts are synchronized
automatically, your expenses categorized and your budgets followed day by day, on the web
and in the iPhone and Android apps.

### Features

- **Automatic bank synchronization** through [woob](https://woob.tech/) (French banks
  mainly), with the strong authentication of the bank; or **import of statements** (OFX,
  QIF, CSV, CAMT.053)
- **Categorization** by keyword rules, **budgets** per category, **insights** and **trends**
  over 24 months, recurring transactions
- **Household**: several users sharing the same accounts, each with their language, theme
  and notifications
- **Mobile apps** for [iPhone](https://apps.apple.com/fr/app/carbure/id6824057363) and
  [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=org.carbure.app), with push
  notifications (large expenses, reached budgets, synchronization results)
- **AI agents** (Claude, ChatGPT…): read-only MCP server
- Two-factor authentication, no third-party cloud: your bank credentials stay on your server
