Carbure is installed at <https://__DOMAIN__/portal/>.

1. Log in with the login **__ADMIN__** and the password chosen at the installation.
2. In **Accounts**, add your bank (**+** button), then choose the accounts to follow.
3. In **My profile → Two-factor authentication**, protect your account.
4. On your phone, install the app ([iPhone](https://apps.apple.com/fr/app/carbure/id6824057363),
   [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=org.carbure.app)), then scan the
   QR code of **My profile → My devices**.

All the accounts are synchronized every day at __SYNC_HOUR__:__SYNC_MINUTE__. Push
notifications are enabled in **Administration → Mobile push**.
